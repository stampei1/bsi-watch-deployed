About

Each new stool sample gives two scores: the model's estimate of the risk of an E. coli and of an Enterococcus bloodstream infection (BSI) in the next 14 days. Patients are grouped by what needs a look today: a new positive blood culture, a High or Watch score that has not been reviewed (or rose to a higher tier since the last review), reviewed, and low risk.

Review burden

Rates are the share of stool samples in each tier followed by that infection within 14 days, in the retrospective MSK allo-HCT cohort. Scores come from models that never saw the patient shown (cross-validation); cut-offs were chosen on the same cohort and have not been validated prospectively. Patients are real de-identified timelines placed together on a simulated unit; bed numbers are made up. Reviews are a demo of the workflow and are stored only in this browser.
